our story

a big dream with an even bigger sweet tooth

Fogue started in a home kitchen with one stand mixer and a stubborn belief that dessert should taste like it was made by someone who loves you. We bake everything from scratch — real butter, real vanilla, no shortcuts.

From silky brigadeiros to towering layer cakes and our famous no-churn ice cream, every order is mixed, baked, and finished by hand the day it goes out.
