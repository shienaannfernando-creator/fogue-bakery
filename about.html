our story

a big dream with an even bigger sweet tooth

Everything you taste here started in one home kitchen, with one stand mixer, and a stubborn belief that dessert should taste like it was made by someone who loves you.

made from scratch, by hand

My journey with baking didn't begin with me but years and years ago when my grandma took her passion and began sharing it with others in her community during her baking classes. My mother, who hated ordering food for dinner parties and insisted we always served homemade food whenever possible to share our culture and effort. And then, to me, a girl who had a huge sweet tooth, and maybe a bigger passion in the kitchen.

I still use the same Wilton tips my grandmother lent me years ago for icing cakes and the same recipes my mother used before I took over the kitchen at home. And now, I hope to share my learnings, both from the kitchen and also the books through this online magazine. An online scrapbook of mine.
