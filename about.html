our story

a big dream with an even bigger sweet tooth

Everything you taste here started in one home kitchen, with one stand mixer, and a stubborn belief that dessert should taste like it was made by someone who loves you.

made from scratch, by hand

We bake everything from scratch — real butter, real vanilla, no shortcuts. From silky brigadeiros to towering layer cakes and our famous no-churn ice cream, every order is mixed, baked, and finished by hand the day it goes out.

Small batches mean we can fuss over the details: browning the butter properly, whipping cream to just the right peak, toasting meringue until it's exactly golden. It's slower. It tastes better. We think that's the whole point.
